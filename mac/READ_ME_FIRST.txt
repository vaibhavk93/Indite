indite for Mac - read me first
==============================

indite turns what you say (Hindi, English, or both mixed) into text, on your Mac. Nothing you say leaves the Mac
unless you use Ask AI.

NEEDS
- A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3 or M4). Not Intel. Check: Apple menu > About This Mac > "Chip".
- macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. Check: Apple menu > About This Mac.
- About 1.2 GB of free space, 8 GB of memory.

INSTALL (2 minutes)
1. Double-click indite-mac.zip, if it is not unzipped already.
2. Drag "indite" (the app) into your Applications folder.
3. The FIRST time only: in Applications, RIGHT-click indite > Open > Open.
   (indite is not from the App Store yet, so macOS asks once. A normal double-click may say it "can't be opened"
   or "can't be checked for malicious software" - right-click > Open avoids that.)
   If macOS still refuses: System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > "Open Anyway".
4. indite lives in the menu bar (top right of the screen, a small microphone). Its window opens by itself the
   first time and walks you through setup:
   - Accessibility: lets indite notice the talk key and type for you.
   - Microphone: lets indite hear you.
   - Screen & System Audio Recording (only for meetings): lets indite hear the other people in a call.
   After switching Accessibility on, quit indite (menu bar icon > Quit) and open it again.

USE
- Hold the Right Option key, speak, let go: the text is typed where your cursor is.
- Open indite from the menu bar for your notes, meetings, files, Dictionary and Settings. Help is inside the app.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- indite shows a message if it cannot start, with the reason.
- The details are in ~/Library/Logs/indite-mac.log (Finder > Go > Go to Folder > paste that path).
- Send that file to the person who gave you indite.
