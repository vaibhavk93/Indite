How it works. Click into any text box — a note, a browser, a chat. Hold the hold-to-talk key, say a sentence, let go. A pill appears above the Dock while you speak and tells you where the words went.

If nothing happens when you hold the key, macOS has not allowed indite to notice key presses. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and switch indite on, then quit and start it again.

If there is no text box to type into, the words go to the clipboard instead of vanishing. The pill says “✓ copied”. Paste with ⌘V.

Privacy. Everything happens on this Mac. The recording is written to a temporary file, transcribed, and deleted straight after. Nothing is sent anywhere — there is no account and no server.

Where your history lives. ~/Library/Application Support/indite — a plain text file you can read or delete yourself.
