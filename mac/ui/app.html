→

When indite keeps writing a word wrong, teach it once. It matches whole words, in any capitals, and a fix can be several words. Used on every dictation, file and meeting.

It also works the other way: put a short word on the left and anything you like on the right, and saying the short word types the whole thing (up to 80 characters): addr → 12 MG Road, Bengaluru 560001, or my mail → naam@example.com.

Tip: you can also fix a word straight from a dictation — “…” → Fix a word.

General

Hold-to-talk key

Hold it anywhere, speak, let go. Changes at once.

Hands-free key

Look & sounds

Appearance

Light is white all day. “Like the Mac” follows your Mac’s light or dark setting.

Show text after dictating

A small card above the pill shows what was written, for up to 6 seconds — keep the pointer on it and it stays. Handy when the words went to the clipboard.

AI buttons on the card

Ask AI and two quick AI actions under the text. Off keeps only To do.

Card buttons

The two AI buttons between Ask AI and To do. Automatic uses the two you ask for most.

Pill transparency

100% is clear glass; lower adds a tint behind the text. The pill shows for a moment as you change it. Arrow keys adjust in 10% steps.

Sounds

A soft tick when listening starts, a pop when it stops.

Writing

Hinglish spelling

Writes everyday words one way: achha, kyunki, maine.

Numbers as digits

“chaar baje” becomes “4 baje” and “do hazaar rupaye” becomes “₹2000”. Words that are also ordinary words (ek, do, saath) change only next to baje, percent, rupaye…

Hide swear words (b****)

Common Hindi and English swear words are written with stars wherever text is shown, typed, copied or exported. The saved note keeps the real word. Off by default.

Also hide these words

Your own, separated by commas. Hidden the same way; the saved note keeps them.

Mark likely mistakes (being tested)

After a dictation, indite looks for your Dictionary words it may have misheard (“cross in” → Crocin) and asks you. Nothing changes until you click Fix. Runs on this Mac.

Speech test

Measure how well indite hears you. On Dictations, fix a note’s text, then ⋯ → “This is exactly what I said”. The filter “To check” lists the rest. Needs “Keep the audio” on; everything stays on this Mac.

Find who spoke

In every meeting and file, automatically. Lines get Person 1, Person 2… which you can rename. If only one person speaks, lines get no name.

Ask to record calls

When a call app (Zoom, Teams, Slack, FaceTime, WhatsApp, Webex, Discord, Skype) starts using the microphone, the card above the pill asks: Record this call? indite never records without your click. Calls in a browser (Google Meet) are not noticed.

Find things

Search

On Dictations, Meetings, Files, To do and the Dictionary. Type several words: all of them must appear, in any order. Put “quotes” around words that must stay together; curly quotes work too. Capitals and accents don’t matter, so cafe finds Café, and codes like JIRA-123 or ₹4,500 are just text. It looks in the words, titles, AI answers, the app a dictation went to and, for meetings, every speaker line. Matches are marked, and the best match comes first. Esc clears it.

Filter

The buttons narrow by what a note is (Starred, With AI, To do, Copied…). The menus under them narrow further: When (Today, the last 7 or 30 days, any month or year you have notes in), App (where a dictation went) and Mistake review (notes with suggestions, or where you chose Fix, Always fix or Not a mistake; recorded from 24 Oct on). A filled menu means a filter is on; “n of total” and Clear show how much is hidden.

Group and sort

Group by date (Today, Yesterday, the weekday, then months with the year), by app, or not at all. Date headings appear when you sort by date; sort by longest, shortest or A–Z and you get one plain list. Sort orders the notes inside each group. Your filters, group and sort are remembered for each page; the search words are not.

Find inside one long transcript

Open a meeting or dictation → Listen. The Find in this transcript box lights every line that has all your words and marks them. Return or ↓ goes to the next line, ⇧Return or ↑ to the previous one. ⌘F jumps to it while Listen is open.

A long history

Lists show 100 notes at a time and load more as you scroll, so years of notes stay quick. By default indite keeps your newest 500 plain dictations; Settings → Privacy → How many to keep raises that, or keeps everything. Starred notes, to-dos, meetings and files are never removed to make room.

Deleting a note that has a recording

“…” → Delete asks what to remove: the note and its recording, the recording only (the words stay) or the words only (the recording stays, as a note that is just sound). Each can be undone for a few seconds. A transcribed file’s original is never touched.

If something is off

Holding the key does nothing

macOS has not let indite notice key presses. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → switch on indite, then quit and reopen it.

The pill says “copied”

There was no text box to type into, so the words went to the clipboard. Paste with ⌘V.

It cannot hear you

Usually a Bluetooth headset that is off. indite tries every microphone, and says so if none work.

The pill says “password box”

indite never types, copies or saves what you say into a password field.

Start indite when you log in

System Settings → General → Login Items → + → choose indite.app.

Set up again

Permissions, the key, and a test.

indite does not open, or closes at once

It shows a message saying why. The details are in ~/Library/Logs/indite-mac.log (Finder → Go → Go to Folder). Send that file to whoever gave you indite.

Give indite to someone else

What their Mac needs

Apple Silicon (Apple menu → About This Mac → Chip says M1, M2, M3 or M4), macOS 14 or newer, about 1.2 GB free. Intel Macs are not supported.

What to send

The file indite-mac.zip (about 630 MB). It holds the app and “READ ME FIRST”. The copy of indite you use every day is not the one to send: it needs this Mac’s project folder.

The first open

Unzip, drag indite to Applications, then right-click it → Open → Open (only the first time: indite is not from the App Store yet). Then allow Accessibility and the Microphone when setup asks.

Privacy

One private dictation

Menu bar icon → “Don’t save the next dictation”. The next one is typed as usual but never saved, copied or logged, and the switch turns itself off. The app you typed into still keeps what you typed.

Everything is transcribed on this Mac

No account, no server. Only Ask AI sends a note’s text out, and only if you turn AI on.

Your files

Notes, dictionary and audio live in ~/Library/Application Support/indite

Set up indite

Four steps and one optional choice, about a minute. Everything stays on this Mac.

  1. 1
    Let indite notice the key and type for you

    System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → switch on indite. Then quit and reopen indite from the menu bar.

  2. 2
    Let indite hear you

    macOS asks once. If you said no before, turn indite on under Privacy & Security → Microphone.

  3. 3
    Pick the key you hold to talk

    One you do not use for anything else.

  4. 4
    Try it

    Click in the box, hold , say “Hello indite, yeh mera pehla test hai”, and let go.

    Works once steps 1 and 2 show ✓.

  5. 5
    Show your words after you speak? optional

    A small card above the pill shows what was written for a few seconds, with Ask AI and To do. It sits over the bottom of the screen, so a click there can land on the card instead of a chat box. Change it any time in Settings → Look & sounds.
